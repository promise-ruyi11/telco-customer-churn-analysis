# Telco Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyses customer churn patterns using the Telco Customer Churn Dataset as part of the AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship Programme – Week 1 Business Analytics Case Study.

## Business Objective
The analysis aims to understand the customer base, identify customer segments with higher churn, examine factors influencing customer retention, and provide actionable recommendations for improving customer retention.

## Dataset
The dataset contains 7,043 customer records and 21 variables covering customer demographics, tenure, services, contract type, payment methods, charges, and churn status.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- Data Visualisation
- PowerPoint

## Key Areas Analysed
- Customer demographics
- Contract type and retention
- Tenure and loyalty
- Internet services and churn
- Payment methods and churn
- Customer churn patterns

## Key Findings
- Month-to-month contract customers have the highest churn.
- Two-year contract customers show stronger retention.
- Electronic check users have the highest churn among payment methods.
- Fiber optic customers show higher churn.
- Customer churn patterns vary across tenure and other customer characteristics.

## Recommendations
- Introduce loyalty incentives for month-to-month customers.
- Encourage customers to adopt longer-term contracts.
- Investigate fiber optic customer experience and service quality.
- Promote alternative payment methods.
- Develop targeted retention campaigns for customers at higher risk of churn.
